This page describes what this local version actually does. Advertising and third-party analytics are disabled.

Public browsing

This public edition is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no visitor login, advertising, third-party analytics or local editorial cookies. Fonts and portraits are served with the site. Searches run in your browser using the published dataset.

Hosting

GitHub processes requests to deliver and secure the website, including visitors’ IP addresses. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Corrections

Corrections are submitted through GitHub Issues. Information you submit there follows GitHub’s privacy rules and may be public. Do not include passwords, private account information or sensitive personal data.

Future advertising

Advertising is currently disabled. If advertising or analytics are added, this notice and applicable consent controls will be updated.

Anonymous feedback

Feedback is sent to Formspree for delivery to the site owner. No visitor account is required. The optional name, message, topic and referring page are sent only when you submit; feedback is not posted publicly. Formspree also processes connection information under its privacy policy.
