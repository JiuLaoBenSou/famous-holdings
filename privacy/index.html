SITE INFORMATION

A small
data footprint.

How this public website, its hosting, feedback service and advertising process information.

Public browsing

This public edition is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no visitor login, separate analytics service or local editorial cookies. Fonts and portraits are served with the site. Searches run in your browser using the published dataset.

Hosting

GitHub processes requests to deliver and secure the website, including visitors’ IP addresses. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Corrections

Corrections are submitted through GitHub Issues. Information you submit there follows GitHub’s privacy rules and may be public. Do not include passwords, private account information or sensitive personal data.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We load Google AdSense to support this free research website. Ad availability depends on Google's site review and advertising settings. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses and device information to serve and measure ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalized advertising in Google My Ad Center and learn how Google uses information from partner sites. Other vendors' personalized advertising choices are available through YourAdChoices. Where offered, use the advertising consent message to manage or withdraw your choices. Browser cookie settings also apply. Advertising choices do not affect access to our research content.

Ads do not determine the ordering of people or the contents of investment records. Advertising is separate from editorial research and is not an investment recommendation.

Anonymous feedback

Feedback is sent to Formspree for delivery to the site owner. No visitor account is required. The optional name, message, topic and referring page are sent only when you submit; feedback is not posted publicly. Formspree also processes connection information under its privacy policy.
