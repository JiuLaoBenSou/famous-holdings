ABOUT / HOLDATLAS

Public records.
Useful perspectives.

An independent research index for curious readers and the creators who explain finance.

Less searching. More understanding.

HoldAtlas brings scattered public investment information into a clear, dated record. Start with a person, identify the actual owner and follow the evidence back to its origin.

We cover institutional portfolios, historical family transactions and dated personal statements. We do not connect to private accounts or offer a real-time holdings feed.

Made for research.

Our aim is to help writers, video creators and readers find a story’s source and understand its limits. Coverage depends on what can be sourced, not on a claim to know every investment.

Independent and free.

The site is free to browse. Advertising may help support it later; advertising is currently disabled. There are no sponsored rankings or investment recommendations.
