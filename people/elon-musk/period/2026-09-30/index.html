Includes (i) 413,152,109 shares held of record by the Elon Musk Revocable Trust dated July 22, 2003; and (ii) 286,428,773 shares of restricted stock that were issued to Mr. Musk upon exercise of the 2018 CEO Performance Award and are subject to a service-based forfeiture condition in accordance with the terms of the Implementation Agreement, but are otherwise issued and outstanding and carry voting rights that may be exercised by Mr. Musk. Amounts do not include 423,743,904 shares of restricted stock granted to Mr. Musk pursuant to the 2025 CEO Performance Award, which shares are subject to a voting agreement (the “Voting Agreement”) and over which an irrevocable proxy has been given to Tesla’s corporate secretary to vote the shares proportionately to the votes of all shares of the Company other than the Unearned Shares, including shares otherwise owned by Mr. Musk, in accordance with the terms of the Voting Agreement. As such, Mr. Musk does not have dispositive or voting control over these shares. Corresponding adjustments have been made to the shares outstanding for purposes of calculating the percentage of shares beneficially owned by Mr. Musk. Includes 207,498,721 shares pledged as collateral to secure certain personal indebtedness. ​
