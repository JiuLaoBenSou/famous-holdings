02 / THE READING GUIDE

Context is
the advantage.

01

Know the owner.

A fund’s positions belong to the fund. A spouse’s trade is a family disclosure. We keep the distinction visible.

02

Check the date.

A quarter-end report and a public statement describe different moments. Neither is a live portfolio.

03

Follow the source.

Every record opens back to its evidence, with source type, dates and the scope of what it supports.
